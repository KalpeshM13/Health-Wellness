Health Assessment System

Complete 3 phases to get your personalized health analysis

Phase 1 of 3

Phase 1: Physical Health

1. How would you rate your overall energy levels throughout the day?

2. How many hours of quality sleep do you get per night on average?

3. How often do you experience physical pain or discomfort?

4. How would you describe your appetite and eating patterns?

5. How often do you engage in physical exercise or activity?

6. How would you rate your immune system strength (frequency of getting sick)?

Phase 2: Mental Health

1. How would you rate your overall mood and emotional well-being?

2. How well do you handle stress and pressure?

3. How would you describe your ability to concentrate and focus?

4. How often do you experience anxiety or worry?

5. How satisfied are you with your social relationships and connections?

6. How would you rate your memory and cognitive function?

Phase 3: Lifestyle & Habits

1. How would you rate your work-life balance?

2. How often do you consume alcohol or use substances?

3. How much time do you spend on screens (phone, computer, TV) daily?

4. How would you rate your hydration habits (water intake)?

5. How often do you take breaks and practice relaxation techniques?

6. How would you rate your overall lifestyle habits and routines?
