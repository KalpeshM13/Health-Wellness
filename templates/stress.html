Guided Box Breathing

This technique is used by Navy SEALs, first responders, and athletes to stay calm under pressure. It helps regulate your autonomic nervous system.

  • 1 Breathe in slowly for 4 seconds
  • 2 Hold your breath for 4 seconds
  • 3 Exhale slowly for 4 seconds
  • 4 Hold empty for 4 seconds

Long-term Relief

Stress Management Solutions

Build lasting resilience with these proven approaches to managing stress.

🧠

Cognitive Reframing

Change how you think about stressors to reduce their impact on your wellbeing.

  • Challenge negative automatic thoughts
  • Ask "What would I tell a friend?"
  • Look for evidence against your fears
  • Practice the ABCDE technique
🏋️

Physical Release

Use movement to release stress hormones and boost feel-good endorphins.

  • 20-minute brisk walking daily
  • Progressive muscle relaxation
  • Shake it out - literally shake your body
  • Stretch tight shoulders and neck
🌙

Sleep Optimization

Quality sleep is your foundation for stress resilience and emotional regulation.

  • Keep consistent sleep/wake times
  • Create a wind-down routine
  • Keep bedroom cool (65-68°F)
  • No screens 1 hour before bed
🤝

Social Connection

Human connection releases oxytocin and provides emotional support.

  • Schedule regular check-ins with friends
  • Join a group or community
  • Practice active listening
  • Don't isolate when stressed
